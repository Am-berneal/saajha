# Field research plan

Tech4Bharat asks teams to take the problem statement into the field, test it, then define the solution. This is our plan, and the place to record what we found.

## Who to meet (minimum)

| Group | How many | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Micro enterprise owners selling B2B (tailors, packaging, small fabricators, traders) | 8–10 | Local industrial area / market |
| Buyers who purchase from micro suppliers (distributor, mid-size manufacturer) | 2 | Purchase or accounts team |
| Chartered Accountants / tax practitioners with micro clients | 2 | Small-town CA firms |
| JanAI Café / CSC / cyber-café operators | 1–2 | Nearest café |

## Questions for micro owners (15 minutes, in their language)

1. How do you make invoices today? Show us one.
2. How many B2B invoices a month? How long does each take?
3. How do you send papers to your CA? How often? How long does it take you?
4. Have you paid a GST late fee or interest in the last year? How much?
5. Do you know what IMS is? Who accepts or rejects purchase bills for you?
6. How many days do your buyers usually take to pay? Have you ever claimed interest for late payment? Why not?
7. Has a buyer ever stopped ordering because of GST filing problems?
8. What phone do you use? Do you use WhatsApp voice notes?
9. Would you send a voice note to create a bill? What would worry you?
10. Have you received a GST notice? What did you do?

## Questions for buyers

1. How many micro or small suppliers do you buy from?
2. In a month, how many of their invoices are missing from your GSTR-2B? How much ITC is delayed?
3. How do you track the 45-day payment rule for micro suppliers?
4. Would you pay a per-supplier fee to keep suppliers compliant and paid on time? How much?

## Questions for CAs

1. How many micro clients? Hours per client per month on data entry?
2. Most common mistakes and notices for micro clients?
3. Would you approve AI-prepared filings in a batch? What would you need to trust it?

## Questions for café operators

1. Which government or GST tasks do people bring to you?
2. How are you paid per task? Would you onboard businesses for a fee?
